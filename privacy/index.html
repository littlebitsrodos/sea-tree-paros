2. When you browse the site

seatree.gr is a static website. It has no contact form, no payment checkout, and it does not use Google Analytics or advertising cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc. / Microsoft). The host may process technical data, such as your IP address, as needed to deliver the page. Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating the site (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The homepage includes an embedded Google Map (Google Maps) in the location section. The map loads on every visit to the homepage. When it loads, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies. Google’s privacy policy applies. We do not send them your name or message.

The availability calendar reads a file of booked dates from this site. It does not send your personal details.

Your browser may keep a copy of pages on your device so the site can open offline. That copy stays on your device.

3. When you contact us

There is no form on this site. If you tap WhatsApp, phone, or email, the conversation starts on your device. If you have picked dates on the calendar, they may be added to the draft message. You send that content yourself.

We use what you send us (for example your name, email, phone number, dates, and message) to reply to your booking enquiry. Legal basis: steps at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

If you choose WhatsApp, WhatsApp / Meta’s policy also applies. If you book through Airbnb or Booking.com, their policies apply. We do not send them your data from this site.

If a booking is completed, we declare the stay to the Greek Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE), in the Short-Term Rental Property Registry (Property Registry Number — AMA), as tax law requires.

4. What we do not do

We do not take payments on seatree.gr. We do not currently use Google Analytics or advertising trackers. We do not profile visitors.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed for its purpose, and as long as the law requires us to keep it (Article 5(1)(e) GDPR).

  • Enquiry messages that do not become a booking: for up to 12 months after the last contact, or we delete them sooner if you ask us to.
  • Completed bookings, invoices and declarations to AADE: for at least five (5) years from the end of the tax year in which the related return is due, and longer if a tax audit or tax debt is outstanding.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent where it was asked for. To exercise a right, email antocosto@gmail.com. We reply within one month, as GDPR requires.

7. Complaints

You can lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, 1-3 Kifisias Ave., 115 23 Athens, tel. +30 210 6475600, dpa.gr.

8. Changes

If we make a material change to this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.
